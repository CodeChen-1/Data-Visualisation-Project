Road Safety Enforcement Dashboard

Visualising patterns in speeding fines, roadside testing, and positive detections across Australia (2020–2024).

About This Project

This dashboard explores national road safety enforcement data between 2020 and 2024, focusing on key aspects such as speeding fines, testing volume, and positive detections. Through interactive D3 visualisations, it aims to uncover patterns across states, age groups, and time.

Each page presents one aspect of the data, supported by a narrative that explains trends and potential factors influencing them.

📍 Choropleth Map

Explore geographic variations in speeding fines and enforcement intensity across Australian states.

📊 Yearly Trends

See how testing and fines evolved from 2020–2024, including impacts of policy and pandemic recovery.

👥 Age Group Breakdown

Compare enforcement and violations across different age categories to identify key risk groups.

⚫ Correlation Analysis

Analyse the relationship between total tests and fines, with bubble size showing positive detections.
